# Rotary District 3110 website: user manual

*For everyone who uses the district website at rotary3110.org: visitors, clubs, members, moderators and the
district's administrators. Updated 24 November 2026.*

The fuller guides for each group are in this folder: the **Moderator guide** (every desk and Sheet task), the
**Google Sheet setup** guide (installing and changing the script), and the **Yearly update** guide (July). This
manual is the one place to start.

---

## 1. How the website works

The website shows what Rotary District 3110's clubs do. Clubs and members send things in through **Google Forms**.
**Moderators** check each item. Only what a moderator approved appears on the website.

| Step | What happens | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Send | A club sends an activity, an event, its club details, a public service, or news about its members and roles. A member sends their own details. | Clubs, members |
| 2. Check | The item waits for a moderator, who approves it, corrects it, or rejects it with a reason. The sender is emailed either way. | Moderators |
| 3. Publish | The website updates **four times a day**: about **9 am, 1 pm, 6 pm and 10 pm** India time, taking everything approved, hidden, removed or corrected since the last update. On **Sundays** it always rebuilds. | Automatic |

Everything lives in two Google Sheets owned by the district account: the **main Sheet** (clubs, activities,
events, office bearers, teams, settings) and the **private members Sheet** (the member list, with mobiles and
emails, and members' requests). The private Sheet is never published. The website reads only approved rows and
public fields.

**In an emergency** (something must come off the website now), an administrator uses **Rotary → Rebuild site now**
in the Sheet; the website updates within about 30 minutes.

## 2. For visitors

### Finding your way

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Home** | This week's highlights, district campaigns and the latest activities |
| **Activities** | Every published club activity, newest first, with photos; filter by club, Rotary year and Avenue of Service |
| **Events** | Coming district and club events, with an add-to-calendar file |
| **Clubs** | Every club with its page: office bearers, activities, meeting day and place, website and social links |
| **Leadership** and **Teams** | The Governor, the district team and committees, with search |
| **Directory** | Members who chose to be listed, by profession and city |
| **Rotary Means Business** | Members' businesses and the topics members can speak on |
| **About** | The District Governor's profile, the Past District Governors (each shown as "PDG"), and a glossary of Rotary terms |
| **Footer links** | Public services (blood banks, eye care, dialysis…), cities, meetings by day, the weekly bulletin, campaigns, news, past years, recognition, the magazine, member spotlight, celebrations, messages, contact, privacy |

- **Search** (the magnifying glass at the top) finds activities, clubs, people and pages.
- **A+** at the top makes the text larger; the phone remembers it.
- **Calendar:** the Events page offers a calendar file (`/events/calendar.ics`) that phones and Outlook can subscribe to.
- **Short links** for sharing in WhatsApp: `/submit/`, `/club-profile/`, `/club-changes/`, `/public-service/`,
  `/consent/` (Show my details), `/update/` (Update my details), `/remove/`, `/list-business/`. Each opens the right form.

### Writing to the district

Use the **Contact** page. Your message goes to the district office; your reply details are seen only by the office
and are never shown on the website. The office replies from the district account.

### Privacy, in short

The website never shows members' phone numbers or email addresses. A member's details appear only when the member
chose List on the Show my details form. People helped by clubs (beneficiaries) are never named. The **Privacy** page
explains it fully.

## 3. For clubs

### Who may send for a club

A club's **President, Secretary and Executive Secretary** speak for the club. Until the club tells the district this
year's holder of a role, **the last known holder carries on** in that role. Next year's office bearers, reported in
advance, are shown as **President Elect**, **Secretary Elect** and **Executive Secretary Elect** and may already send
the club's activities and club updates; they take over on 1 July.

The district may switch on **"only office bearers may send"**. Then the activity form and the Club updates form are
accepted for your club only from your acting office bearers, sent from the Google account whose email the district
has for them. Anyone else gets a polite email saying who can send; nothing is lost, and a moderator can let it
through. **If you are a new office bearer**, tell the district through Club updates (members and roles), or reply to
that email so a moderator can add you.

### Sending an activity or event (about two minutes)

Open the **activity form** (`/submit/`, or your club's own link from the district office) and choose:

- **An activity we did:** title, date, area of focus, Avenue of Service, a short description, **1 to 3 photos** (each
  under 1 MB) or a link to your club's own post, and the photo consent tick. Optional: people helped, amount spent,
  partner clubs, a district campaign and your count, a video link, a newspaper report, "district level", and one
  **before** and one **after** photo of a place.
- **An upcoming event:** title, type, dates and time, venue and city, organiser, description, registration closing
  date and fee, whether it is open to everyone, a map and details link, photos.

**What moderators check:** a photo or link is there; photo consent is ticked; no phone numbers, emails or children's
names in the text; the date is not in the future; it is not a duplicate; a "district level" mark is right; a
campaign count is reasonable.

**Good photos:** people doing the work, taken across (landscape), one clear photo rather than a collage; everyone in
it (or a child's parent) agreed. Before and after photos show a place, never people. The website removes the
location phones store in photos.

**Good text:** say what was done in the title ("Free eye camp for 220 villagers"), then two to five lines: what, for
whom, with whom, and what came of it. The **How to post** page (`/how-to-post/`) has more tips.

You are emailed when it is approved (with its link, to share in your club's WhatsApp group) or not published (with
the reason; you can send it again).

### Club updates (one form, three sections)

Page 1 asks your club and who you are, then what you are updating:

| Section | Use it for | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Club details** | Website, social media links, meeting day and place, permanent projects, regular services | A moderator checks it; approved details replace what the club page shows. A blank answer keeps what is there; "none" clears a list. |
| **A member or a role** | A member joined, left or passed away; a President, Secretary or Executive Secretary; a district role. A member who moves: the old club sends "left", the new club "joined" (the "joined" waits until the "left" is in). | A Members moderator checks it against the member list. A new member's mobile and email may be given with "joined"; they stay private, and an email another member already uses is not saved. |
| **A public service** | A lasting facility the public can use: blood bank, eye care, dialysis, ambulance… (one form per facility) | A moderator checks the facility's own phone number (never a member's mobile) and publishes it under Public services. |

### The weekly club email

When the district switches it on, your club's office bearers get **one email a week**, on your club's own weekday,
listing what the district approved for your club that week: activities and events published, club details and
public services, members and roles, and members who chose to show their details. It ends with the office bearers
the district has on record for your club; if they are out of date, send Club updates. No email comes in a week when
nothing happened.

### Reminders

A club with no activity in 30 days may get a friendly reminder from the district's Club engagement moderator, by
email to the office bearers or on WhatsApp, with the club's own form link. While the activity form is paused (an
administrator's choice), the website shows no way to send activities or events, and no reminders go out.

## 4. For members

### Your details: two forms (since 23 November 2026)

| Form | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Show my details** | What the website may show about you: **List** or **Do not list** for each of directory listing (your name and club), profession lines (profession, area, what you do, website), photo, birthday, wedding anniversary, couple photo, Call button, WhatsApp button and member spotlight. Birthdays and anniversaries show as day and month only. Your email is never shown, and your numbers only as the buttons you chose. To take something off, send it again with Do not list. A photo you stop listing stays in the district's private Google Drive, not shown, so you can list it again without sending it again. |
| **Update my details** | The details themselves; fill in only what should change: profession, area, what you do, website, the numbers for your Call and WhatsApp buttons (blank: the mobile Rotary has), a **second email** (if the one Rotary has is old), birthday, anniversary and spouse, photo and couple photo. At the end, list or update your business and the topics you can speak on (Rotary Means Business), or remove your business listing. |

**Sent from your own Google account** (the email the district has for you, or your second email), it goes in at the
next website update and you get an email. You are found **only by that email**: if the district does not have it,
nothing is applied and you are told to ask your club to add it as your second email.

**Your club's President, Secretary or Executive Secretary** can fill in either form for you. They give your **Rotary
member ID** (that is how you are found), a Members moderator checks it first, and you are told at every email the
district has for you, so you can object. Neither form ever creates a new member: new members come from the club's
"A member joined" or Rotary International's member list.

### Member cards

A member who holds a role this year, or chose a directory listing, has a **member card**: name, club, roles across
the years, and what they chose to show. Names on the Leadership, Teams and club pages link to it. Phone numbers and
emails never appear; the WhatsApp button appears only when the member asked for it.

### Each July

Members who are listed get one email in July or August showing what the website shows about them, with the link to
change or remove it.

## 5. For moderators

### The five types

| Type | Looks after |
|---|---|
| **Content** | Activities, events, club details, public services, business listings; club office bearers |
| **Members** | Members' requests (show, remove, business), club changes (members and roles), district roles, the member list |
| **Club engagement** | Quiet clubs: reminding clubs that have not posted |
| **District office** | Contact messages: reading and replying |
| **Administrator** | Everything above, plus clubs and moderators |

A Content moderator may be given **areas** (zones): they are emailed only about those clubs and see them first.
Areas are never shown on the website.

### Working from email

Each new item sends the right moderators an email with its details and photos. Content items have **Approve** and
**Reject** buttons that open the item on the desk, where one more tap confirms. They work only on a phone where you
are signed in to the desk; anywhere else the desk asks for your sign-in code first. Please do not forward these emails. Members' requests are never approved from an email: open the
desk.

### The moderation desk (made for phones)

Open it from **Open the moderation desk** in any moderator email; your phone remembers it. **Sign in** with the
email on the moderators list: a 6-digit code and a Sign in button are emailed to you (each works once, within 15 minutes) and you stay signed in for 7 days.
**Sign out** on someone else's phone. **English / हिन्दी** switches the desk's own words. The **Guide** button opens
a short guide for each type.

| Tab | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **To check** | **Clean**: activities where the checks found nothing; glance, untick anything wrong, then **Approve the ticked**. **Needs a look**: each item as a card with the reason first; **Approve**, **Reject** (with ready reasons, sent to the sender) or **Correct** (fix spelling, remove a phone number). |
| **Recent** | Approved activities of the last 30 days and coming events: **Story of the month**, **Leave out of the bulletin**, **Hide** (takes it off the website, with a reason) and **Put back**. |
| **Members** | Show my details and Update my details forms a club filled in for a member, old removal requests and club changes, with who sent them; **Approve**, **Reject**, **Remove now**. **Find a member** (name, RI ID, email, second email or mobile) and **Edit** with a reason (the RI ID never changes; an email another member uses is refused); **Mark as deceased**. |
| **Not accepted** | Forms refused because the sender is not an office bearer of the club (only when that check is on). **Let through** sends one to the moderators as usual. |
| **Roles** | How many clubs have told this year's office bearers; roles to check; **Find a role**, **Change**, **End role**, **Add an office bearer**; **Team lists**: **Check** a coming year's district team or club office bearers prepared in the Team lists Sheet, then an Administrator **Imports** it (all or nothing, only with no errors) or **Withdraws** one before its year starts; **Recent changes** with **Undo**. |
| **Quiet clubs** | Clubs with no activity in 30 days; **Email the office bearers** or **WhatsApp**. |
| **Messages** | Contact messages; **Reply** (sent from the district account, signed "Rotary District 3110") or **Mark answered**. |
| **Admin** | Add, rename and close clubs; add and change moderators and their areas. |

**Every correction asks for a reason** and is written to the private change log (who, when, old and new value,
why). **Undo** puts the old value back while nobody has changed it since. What you approve, hide or correct shows on
the website at its next update.

### Things to look out for

- **District level:** the district organised it, or it was a district occasion. If not, change it before approving.
- **Campaign counts** go into the district's totals: check large counts and that only the club's own work is counted.
- **A passing away** sent through Club updates needs **I have confirmed this with the club** ticked before Approve.
- **A sender who is not an office bearer:** while the district is trying the check out ("watch"), the card shows a
  **Sender** line, and the Clean list says so in red.
- **Sent by:** the sender's name, role and mobile come from the members list and the club's office bearers, found by
  the email Google collects (the club forms no longer ask them). An email the members list does not have is shown
  as it is.
- **Within 48 hours:** items waiting longer bring a reminder email at 7 am.

### Practice round (new moderators)

An administrator starts a practice round (**Rotary → Administrator → Practice round: start…**). Practice items appear
on the desk only, never on the website. Each type has a few tasks; the administrator reads the report and removes
the practice items afterwards.

### Never

Never publish phone numbers, emails or children's names; never name beneficiaries; never approve photos without the
consent tick; never use the Sheet's Reject to remove many rows at once (it emails each sender).

## 6. For administrators

### The Rotary menu in the main Sheet

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Approve / Reject selected row(s)** | The same as the desk, from the Sheet |
| **Member requests…** | A side panel with members' requests |
| **Mark as deceased…** | Stops greetings at once |
| **Refresh public members** | Rebuilds the public member list from the private one |
| **Rebuild site now** | The website updates within about 30 minutes (emergencies) |
| **Refresh dashboard** | Recounts clubs' activities |
| **Check roles and member IDs** | Runs the role check (also daily) |
| **Update form lists** | Clubs, areas, professions and campaigns in the forms' drop-downs (also daily) |
| **Pause activity form… / Reopen activity form…** | Closes or reopens the activity and event form; the website hides or shows every button and link to it within about 30 minutes, and the quiet-club reminders stop or start again |
| **Administrator → …** | Data link details, **Upgrade Sheet**, Apply moderators (Sheet access), Sign everyone out of the desk, send the bulletin preview, bulletin message, clubs-not-posting email or weekly summary now, a sample July member email, **Back up both Sheets now**, the practice round, Run setup again |

### Settings (the main Sheet's settings tab)

Change only the **value** column; the notes explain each setting. The ones you are most likely to change:

| Setting | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `club_senders_only` | **off / watch / on**: whether only a club's office bearers may send its forms. Watch refuses nobody and shows who sent each form. |
| `weekly_club_email` | **off / test / on**: the weekly email to each club's office bearers. Test sends the day's club emails to one address instead. |
| `weekly_club_email_test_to` | Where test emails go (blank: the district account) |
| `summary_emails` | Who gets the Monday weekly summary |
| `team_lists_url` | The Team lists Sheet (made by Upgrade Sheet) where the district prepares a coming year's team; share it only with the people who prepare the lists |
| `contact_daily_limit` | How many contact messages are emailed on per day |
| `campaign_check_above` | A campaign count above this gets a note for the moderator |
| `bulletin_start_date` | The Sunday the bulletin archive starts |
| `activity_form` | **open / paused**: set by **Pause activity form** and **Reopen activity form** (use the menu, which also closes or opens the form itself) |

The **moderators** tab names each moderator: type, name, Google email, active, areas. Setting **active** to FALSE
locks that person out of the desk at once.

### What happens by itself (India time)

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Daily, 7 am | 48-hour reminders, the role check, member counts, the forms' lists, the weekly club emails (when on), in July and August the member check emails |
| Every 15 minutes | The website checks whether **Rebuild site now** was pressed |
| 9 am, 1 pm, 6 pm, 10 pm | Website update, when anything changed |
| Saturday, 6 pm | Preview of Sunday's bulletin to Content moderators (leave something out from the email) |
| Sunday, 3 am | Backup of both Sheets (8 weeks kept, private Backups folder) |
| Sunday, 8 am | The bulletin message, to forward to the district's groups; the website always rebuilds on Sunday |
| Monday, 8 am | The weekly summary: what went live, what waits, quiet clubs, roles to check, who sent the club forms |
| 1st of the month, 9 am | Clubs not posting, to the Club engagement moderators |

Google allows a free account about 100 emails a day; the script keeps room for moderators and replies, and moves
anything left (weekly club emails, July member checks) to the next day.

### When the script changes

1. Paste the new `Code.gs` into **Extensions → Apps Script**, save.
2. **Deploy → Manage deployments → ✏ edit → Version: New version → Deploy** (the address stays the same).
3. Reload the Sheet and run **Rotary → Administrator → Upgrade Sheet** when the change says so. It adds new tabs,
   columns, settings and form questions, and is safe to run twice.

Google limits worth knowing: a tab still linked to a form cannot be deleted (in the Sheet: **Form → Unlink form**
first); Google sometimes refuses a change to a form ("Invalid data updating form").

### Each July

The website works out the Rotary year from the date, so on 1 July it moves on by itself: next year's office bearers and
team become this year's and the Governor who has just finished becomes a Past District Governor. The owner starts a
build that morning. Next year's team may be added any time before, best through the **Team lists** Sheet (checked
and imported on the desk); it shows at once, as "Elect". Every year's
Leadership and Teams pages stay, chosen from the **Rotary year** drop-down. The **Yearly update** guide has every step.

## 7. When something is not right

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| My activity is not on the website | Was it approved (you got an email)? It shows at the next update (9 am, 1 pm, 6 pm, 10 pm). Not approved after two days: ask the district office. |
| "Not accepted: only a club's office bearers can send this" | Ask your President, Secretary or Executive Secretary to send it, or tell the district who your new office bearers are (Club updates, members and roles). |
| Something must come off the website now | A moderator **hides** it on the desk, or an administrator uses **Rebuild site now** after removing it. |
| A member's details are wrong | The member sends Update my details, or a Members moderator corrects it on the desk (**Find a member → Edit**; the Rotary member ID never changes). |
| "Your form was not applied": the member's email is not on file | The club adds it as the member's second email on Update my details (a moderator approves it); then the member sends the form again from that email. |
| A photo does not show | It must be a normal photo file under 1 MB; HEIC photos from iPhones usually work but can be slow. Send it again if it is missing after the next update. |
| I cannot sign in to the desk | Your email must be an **active** row of the moderators tab. Codes last 15 minutes; ask for a new one. |
| The website has not updated | Updates run four times a day; GitHub sometimes starts them 15 to 60 minutes late. If a change is still missing after 13 hours, the Administrators are emailed "a rebuild has not happened", once a day until it catches up: see GitHub → Actions. |

## 8. Words used on the website

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Office bearers** | A club's President, Secretary and Executive Secretary |
| **Acting office bearer** | This year's holder, or the last known holder until this year's is told |
| **Elect, Nominee, Nominee Designate** | A role held next year, in two years, in three years ("District Governor Elect") |
| **Past** | Only for a District Governor or a club President whose year is over |
| **Moderator** | A district volunteer who checks items before they are published |
| **The desk** | The moderation desk at `/desk/`, for moderators on their phones |
| **Clean / Needs a look** | Activities with nothing to check / with something a moderator must look at |
| **Area** | A group of clubs a moderator looks after (zones, never shown on the website) |
| **Bulletin** | The district's weekly round-up, published each Sunday |
| **Rotary year** | 1 July to 30 June, written 2026-27 |
| **RI member ID** | The member's number with Rotary International; it joins a person's roles into one card |
